And a bonus, because this one is almost unbelievable. Jaguar sold zero cars in Australia in September 2026. Not a few. Zero.

That's because Jaguar stopped selling new petrol cars here on the 31st of December 2025, as it reinvents itself as an all-electric luxury brand. Its new EVs are expected to cost around three hundred thousand dollars locally, and there's still no confirmed Australian launch date. Jaguar's local retailers are still servicing cars and honouring warranties. But for now, a brand with nearly a century of history is selling nothing new in this country.

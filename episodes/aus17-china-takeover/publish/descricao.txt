42,045 new vehicles built in China were sold in Australia in September 2026 – more than Japan and Thailand combined. China overtook Japan as Australia's biggest source of new cars in February 2026, for the first time since 1998, and Chinese-built vehicles now make up almost 4 in 10 new-car sales. Here's how the takeover happened month by month, the brands winning (BYD, Geely, Chery, Zeekr) and losing (Toyota, Mazda, Mitsubishi, Subaru, Nissan), why Australians are switching – and the risks nobody's talking about.

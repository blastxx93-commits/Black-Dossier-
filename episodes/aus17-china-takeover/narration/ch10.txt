So, did Chinese cars just take over Australia?

By country of manufacture, yes. China overtook Japan as Australia's biggest source of new cars in February 2026. By September, almost four in ten new vehicles sold here were built in China, and four of the top ten models were Chinese. BYD is now second only to Toyota. Geely, Chery and Zeekr are growing at rates the industry has never seen.

And it's not over. The Australian Automotive Dealer Association has forecast that Chinese-made vehicles could make up 58 per cent of new cars on Australian roads by 2035. The number of Chinese models on sale is set to almost double by 2027.

But this takeover will also have casualties – including some of the Chinese brands themselves. And for the biggest brand of all, the pressure has never been higher.

Tesla has told a federal inquiry that petrol, diesel and hybrid drivers should pay a per-kilometre road charge – while keeping the 53.7c/litre fuel excise. EV groups, dealers and motoring clubs want a road-user charge too, and NSW already has a law for an EV charge from July 2027. Here's what's being proposed, who wants it, what it could cost you in a petrol car, a ute or an EV, how New Zealand and the UK do it, and the arguments on both sides.

No federal road-user charge has been legislated. Cost examples in this video are illustrations based on published proposals, not confirmed prices.

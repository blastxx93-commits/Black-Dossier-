Now the prices. According to CarExpert and Cars24, these are the H7 drive-away prices at launch.

The H7 Lux hybrid: $45,990 drive-away, rising to $46,990 after the 30th of November 2026.

The H7 Lux plug-in hybrid: $49,990 drive-away, rising to $50,990 after the 30th of November.

The higher-spec Vanta hybrid: $49,990 drive-away. And the Vanta plug-in hybrid: $53,990 drive-away.

The Lux gets 19-inch wheels, a 15.6-inch touchscreen and a head-up display. The Vanta adds a locking rear differential, a panoramic sunroof, heated and ventilated front seats and ambient lighting. A seven-seat version is due in 2027.

And GWM has been sharpening prices across its whole range. In October 2026, CarsGuide reported the Haval Jolion Premium at $23,990 drive-away, $3,000 off, and the Haval H6 hybrid Lux at $37,990, also $3,000 off. So the H7's launch pricing fits a clear strategy: lots of car for the money, with the price including on-road costs.

Now, one important detail. Those H7 prices are drive-away. Most Toyota prices are quoted before on-road costs. On-road costs – things like stamp duty, registration and CTP insurance – can add thousands of dollars, depending on your state. So when you compare the two, you have to compare drive-away with drive-away. Otherwise, the gap looks smaller than it really is.

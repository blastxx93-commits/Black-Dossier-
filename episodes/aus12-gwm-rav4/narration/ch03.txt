Now let's look at the champion.

The Toyota RAV4 is one of the best-selling cars in Australia. And when Toyota added its first-ever RAV4 plug-in hybrid in mid-2026, it took off. According to CarExpert, plug-in hybrids made up thirty-nine per cent of all RAV4 sales in July 2026 – around 2,170 cars. That made the RAV4 PHEV the best-selling plug-in hybrid in the country that month, ahead of the BYD Shark 6.

Here's the current RAV4 plug-in hybrid range, before on-road costs. The XSE front-wheel drive starts at $58,840. The XSE all-wheel drive is $63,340. And the GR Sport all-wheel drive is $66,340.

It uses a 2.5-litre petrol engine with electric motors, producing 201 kilowatts in front-wheel drive form, and Toyota claims an electric-only range of 121 kilometres.

And the regular RAV4 hybrid? CarExpert listed the RAV4 GX two-wheel-drive hybrid from $45,990 before on-road costs. So the H7 Lux hybrid's launch price of $45,990 drive-away matches the cheapest RAV4 hybrid's sticker price – but includes on-road costs and all-wheel drive.

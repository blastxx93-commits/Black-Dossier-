The 2027 GWM Haval H7 plug-in hybrid launches in Australia at $49,990 drive-away – with 320kW, all-wheel drive and a claimed 137km of electric range. The cheapest Toyota RAV4 plug-in hybrid starts at $58,840 before on-road costs. So did GWM just undercut Toyota? In this video I compare the two properly: price, power, electric range, towing, warranty, insurance, resale value and dealer network – plus Toyota's cheaper RAV4 GXL PHEV coming in early 2027.

Prices are as published at launch and can change. The RAV4 GXL PHEV price is a CarExpert estimate, not an official Toyota figure. The running-cost example uses illustrative numbers.

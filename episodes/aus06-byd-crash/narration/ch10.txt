So here's the checklist I promised. Save this one.

Before a crash happens: check your policy. Does it cover a hire car, and for how long? Is the hire car capped at a dollar amount or a number of days? Can you choose your own repairer, or does the insurer choose? And find out where your nearest BYD-approved collision repairer actually is.

When you renew: don't just accept the renewal price. Get quotes from at least three insurers. With differences of hundreds of dollars between companies for the same car, a twenty-minute search can pay for itself many times over. Ask how they handle EV battery damage, and whether they have a specialist EV claims process.

After a crash: take photos of everything, including the dashboard warnings. Tell the tow operator it's an electric or plug-in vehicle. Ask the insurer or repairer for a written estimate of the timeline. Keep a diary of every call and email, with dates. And if the car has been hit near the battery, ask whether a battery health check has been done, and get a copy of the result.

And one more thing to check on your policy: is your car insured for an agreed value or a market value? With an agreed value, you and the insurer settle on the amount up front. With market value, the insurer pays what the car is worth at the time of the crash. If an EV is written off because of battery damage, that difference can matter a lot – especially in a market where used EV prices have been falling. So read the policy wording, not just the price.

It's also worth knowing who chooses the repairer. Some policies let you pick your own workshop, while others send you to the insurer's own network. If you want an EV-certified or BYD-approved repairer, make sure your policy allows it before you need it.

And if you're still deciding whether to buy a BYD, or any EV, ask the dealer three questions before you sign. Where is the nearest approved collision repairer? How long are service bookings right now? And are crash parts for this model stocked in Australia? The answers will tell you a lot about what your life looks like after a crash.

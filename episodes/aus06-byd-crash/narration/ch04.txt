To be fair to BYD, its battery design does help here. BYD uses what it calls the Blade battery, a lithium iron phosphate design. Back in 2024, BYD Australia's collision and repair representatives explained that the Blade battery is built in a way that can, in some cases, allow damaged sections to be repaired rather than replacing the whole pack. Lithium iron phosphate cells are also generally considered more stable and less prone to thermal runaway than some other battery chemistries.

That's genuinely good news for owners. But it only helps if the repairer is trained to do it, has the parts, and the insurer agrees that a repair is safe. And that brings us to the part almost every BYD owner talks about.

Number two: the BYD Sealion 7.

The Sealion 7 is a mid-size electric SUV, and it's become one of the most important cars in Australia.

In the first nine months of 2026, it sold 19,964 cars, up 135 per cent, and finished fifth in the whole country – above the Isuzu D-Max, the Hyundai Kona and the Ford Everest. In the first half of the year, it was BYD's best-selling model, with 12,516 sales, up 233 per cent. And in October, it was fifth again, with 2,524 sales.

The Sealion 7 gives buyers a big electric SUV with a lot of technology at a price that undercuts many rivals – and right now, BYD is adding four thousand dollars cashback, bringing the Sealion 7 Premium to $50,990 before on-road costs.

And it carried BYD to a place no Chinese brand had been before. BYD finished second in the national brand rankings every month from April to October. In October, it sold 10,025 cars, up 153 per cent. The Sealion 7 is the car that made that possible.

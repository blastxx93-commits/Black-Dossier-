Number three: the Geely EX5.

This is the car from the start of the video. In September 2026, Geely EX5 sales were up 620 per cent on September last year. In July, it was up 315 per cent and ninth in the national rankings. And in October, it was the tenth best-selling vehicle in the country, with 1,789 sales, up more than 420 per cent.

Geely as a brand sold 23,632 cars in the first nine months of the year, up 639 per cent. In October alone, it sold 4,365, up 520 per cent. In May, GoAuto reported that Geely had passed ten thousand sales in Australia.

The EX5 is a mid-size electric SUV, priced at $41,990 before on-road costs, and right now Geely is offering novated lease discounts on top. That makes it one of the cheapest mid-size electric SUVs you can buy.

Geely isn't a small company, either. It owns Volvo and Zeekr, it controls Polestar and Lotus, and its founder is one of the biggest shareholders in Mercedes-Benz. So the EX5 isn't a cheap gamble from an unknown brand – it's one of the biggest car groups in the world finally getting serious about Australia.

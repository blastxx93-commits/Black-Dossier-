Number ten: the BYD Atto 2.

The Atto 2 is a small SUV, and it's one of BYD's newest models in Australia. And it took off almost straight away.

In the first half of 2026, the Atto 2 sold 5,401 cars, according to CarExpert – putting it in a close race for BYD's third-best seller. And in October, it was the eighth best-selling vehicle in the entire country, with 1,943 sales. A brand-new nameplate, inside the national top ten.

Part of the reason is price. BYD now offers a plug-in hybrid Atto 2 DM-i at $25,990 drive-away. That's cheaper than many petrol small SUVs, with much lower fuel bills.

The Atto 2 shows how BYD wins: not with one hero car, but with a whole wall of models at every price point. If buyers don't want the Atto 2, there's the Atto 1 for $19,990 drive-away, or the Sealion 5, 6, 7 or 8. And that's exactly why BYD has become the number two brand in Australia.

Number eight: the Toyota Prado.

Here's a comeback story. In early 2026, Prado sales fell hard. Toyota said that was mainly about supply, not demand. In the first five months of the year, Prado sales were down 45 per cent.

Then the supply came back – and so did the buyers. In October 2026, the Prado was the sixth best-selling vehicle in Australia, with 2,397 sales, up 64 per cent on the same month last year. By the end of October, the Prado had moved ahead of the Ford Everest for the year, with 18,062 sales against the Everest's 17,944.

That matters, because the Everest has been the Prado's biggest rival, and for a while it looked like Ford was winning that fight.

The new Prado is bigger, tougher and more modern than the old one, and Australians clearly still want a proper LandCruiser-badged four-by-four for towing and touring. It's proof that even in the middle of the Chinese boom, a strong nameplate with the right product can still win.

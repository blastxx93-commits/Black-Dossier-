Number four: the Toyota HiLux.

Australia's favourite ute is back on top. In October 2026, the HiLux was the best-selling vehicle in the whole country, with 5,954 sales, up 34 per cent on the same month last year. It beat the Ford Ranger by more than a thousand units.

That's a big deal, because it had been a tough year. The Ranger led the market for most of 2026, and in the first nine months, HiLux sales were down about ten per cent. The new-generation HiLux arrived during the year, and buyers were waiting for it.

And Toyota is fighting harder than usual. It's offering a four-thousand-dollar finance deposit bonus on the HiLux SR5 – a rare move for Toyota. The new range also includes an electric version.

Now, the HiLux isn't growing the way the Chinese brands are. But it shows that the old champions aren't finished. When Toyota gets the product and the supply right, Australians still line up for it. And the battle between the HiLux and the Ranger for the number one spot in 2026 is going right down to the wire.

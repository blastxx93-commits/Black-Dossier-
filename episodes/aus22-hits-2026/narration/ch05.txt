Number seven: the Chery Tiggo 4.

Two years ago, most Australians had never heard of Chery. In 2026, its little Tiggo 4 is one of the most popular cars in the country.

In the first nine months of the year, the Tiggo 4 sold 19,865 cars, up 43 per cent. That's sixth in the whole country – and ahead of the Hyundai Kona, which sold 18,533. In the first half of the year, it was ahead of both the Kona and the Haval Jolion.

The reason is price. Right now, the Tiggo 4 Urban is $23,990 drive-away, with a $1,500 fuel card, according to the deals tracker CarSorted. That's thousands of dollars less than most of the small SUVs it's beating.

And Chery as a brand is flying. In the first half of 2026, Chery's sales were up 77 per cent, and almost every model it sells grew. In October, the Tiggo 4 was again inside the national top ten, with 2,178 sales.

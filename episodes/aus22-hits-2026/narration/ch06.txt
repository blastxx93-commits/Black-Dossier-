Number six: the Jaecoo J5.

Jaecoo is a sister brand to Chery and Omoda, and it's barely been in Australia long enough for most people to learn how to say it. But its J5 small SUV has gone off like a rocket.

According to ACM's motoring team, the J5 sold more than eleven thousand cars in the first nine months of 2026. And the Omoda Jaecoo group was up 621 per cent for the year to September.

Why? Price and style. The J5 petrol launched at $25,990 drive-away, and the electric J5 EV was priced at $36,990 drive-away. It has a boxy, upmarket look that some people say reminds them of a much more expensive European SUV. The J5 EV had more than four thousand orders within months of launch, according to The Beep.

The J5 shows how fast a new brand can grow in Australia now. A few years ago, a new brand would have taken a decade to build a following. Jaecoo did it in about a year.

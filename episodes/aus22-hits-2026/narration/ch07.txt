Number five: the Zeekr 7X.

Zeekr is Geely's premium electric brand. It's competing against Tesla, BMW, Mercedes and Audi – and in 2026, it's beating a lot of them.

In the first nine months of the year, Zeekr's sales were up 1,560 per cent to 12,120 cars. In October alone, Zeekr sold 1,874 cars, up 767 per cent. And the star is the Zeekr 7X, a mid-size electric SUV. The Driven reported in May that it had become Australia's best-selling premium SUV, and in July it cracked the national top ten for the first time.

Think about how remarkable that is. A premium electric SUV, from a brand that only launched in Australia recently, outselling famous European luxury names.

The 7X wins on technology and value: long range, fast charging, a luxurious cabin, and a price that undercuts many European rivals. And while the brands that were here first have been cutting prices to keep up, Zeekr has been taking orders. If you want to know where the luxury car market in Australia is heading, look at this car.

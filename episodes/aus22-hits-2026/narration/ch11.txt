And number one: the Tesla Model Y.

In the first nine months of 2026, Tesla delivered 35,930 Model Ys in Australia, according to the Electric Vehicle Council. That's up 108 per cent. It finished third in the country – just behind the Ford Ranger and the Toyota HiLux, and ahead of the Toyota RAV4.

Think about that. An electric car is now outselling the RAV4 for the year in Australia. And in May and June, according to CarExpert, the Model Y was the best-selling new vehicle in the entire country – ahead of every ute.

In October, the Model Y sold 3,105, up 322 per cent, and it was the fourth best-selling vehicle in Australia.

It hasn't been perfect. Tesla has been cutting prices on stock, and Tesla as a brand had a quieter October than September. But the Model Y is the reason electric cars stopped being a niche in Australia. When an EV sits on the national podium for the year, the market has changed for good. That's why the Model Y is number one.

So what do these ten winners have in common?

First, value. Almost every car on this list gives buyers more technology, more efficiency or more equipment for less money than the car it's replacing.

Second, choice of power. Hybrids, plug-in hybrids and electric cars are all on this list. Australians didn't all switch to EVs – they switched to whatever saves them money.

Third, the old champions can still fight back. The HiLux and the Prado prove that when Toyota gets supply and product right, buyers return.

And what does this mean for you? Competition is the best thing that can happen to car buyers. The success of these cars is the reason we're seeing four-thousand-dollar cashbacks, fuel cards, and a new car for under twenty thousand dollars drive-away. If you're buying, shop across brands, compare the drive-away price, and don't assume the badge you've always bought is the best value anymore.

So that's the list: the BYD Atto 2, the Haval Jolion, the Toyota Prado, the Chery Tiggo 4, the Jaecoo J5, the Zeekr 7X, the Toyota HiLux, the Geely EX5, the BYD Sealion 7 – and at number one, the Tesla Model Y.

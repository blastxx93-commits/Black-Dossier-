Number nine: the GWM Haval Jolion.

While other brands struggled, the Jolion just kept growing. In the first nine months of 2026, it sold 17,051 cars, up 20 per cent, and it finished ninth in the national year-to-date rankings.

That puts it above some very big names. It's ahead of the Mazda CX-5, the Kia Sportage and the Toyota Corolla Cross in the year-to-date top ten list – none of them made it.

The reason is value. Right now, the Jolion Premium Hybrid is $32,990 drive-away, with GWM adding a three-thousand-dollar deposit contribution until the end of November. That's a lot of small SUV, with a hybrid engine, for the price of a basic petrol rival.

And the Jolion shows something important: not every Chinese success is electric. Plenty of Australians want a cheap, efficient hybrid that works just like their old petrol car – and the Jolion gives them exactly that. GWM as a brand has had a tougher few months, but the Jolion has been its rock.

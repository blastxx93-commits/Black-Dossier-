BYD went from one model to one of Australia's biggest car brands in just a few years – but after-sales support didn't keep up. Owners waited months for parts, workshops lacked EV training, and a 32,009-ute Shark 6 recall added pressure. Then, in October 2026, BYD announced 580,000 parts and $25 million of stock in Australia. Here's the honest story: what went wrong, what BYD repairs and insurance really cost, your rights under Australian Consumer Law, and three questions to ask before you buy.

Number seven: the Toyota HiLux.

This one is rare. Toyota doesn't need to discount much, because Toyota sells more cars than anyone in Australia. In October, the HiLux was the best-selling vehicle in the country, with 5,954 sales.

But the ute market is getting crowded. So according to CarExpert, Toyota is offering a four-thousand-dollar finance deposit bonus on HiLux SR5 four-by-four dual-cab pickups and cab-chassis models – diesel only.

With the bonus, CarExpert says the effective prices before on-road costs are $59,990 for the manual dual-cab pickup, $60,490 for the automatic cab-chassis, and $61,990 for the automatic dual-cab pickup.

Now, here's the catch – and it's a big one. You have to finance the car through Toyota Finance, and you have to borrow at least ten thousand dollars. Leases don't count. And the electric HiLux isn't included. You'll need to order and apply for finance by the 31st of December, with settlement by the end of February.

So if you were going to pay cash, this deal isn't really for you. But if you were going to finance anyway, compare Toyota's interest rate with your bank's. A four-thousand-dollar bonus can disappear quickly if the interest rate is higher.

Number two: the cheapest new car in Australia.

The BYD Atto 1 Essential is now $19,990 drive-away. That's a brand-new, fully electric small car for under twenty thousand dollars, on the road. Zecar describes it as Australia's cheapest new car, and says the saving is between $5,772 and $7,204, depending on your state.

The offer runs for orders until the 15th of December, with delivery by the 31st of December. BYD is also offering the Dolphin Essential electric hatch at $28,888 drive-away, and the Atto 2 plug-in hybrid at $25,990 drive-away.

Think about what that means. For years, the cheapest new cars in Australia were small petrol hatchbacks. Now, according to Zecar, the cheapest new car you can buy is electric, and it's made in China.

The catch? The Atto 1 is a small city car. It's not a family SUV, and its range and space are built for the city, not for long country drives. If most of your driving is in town, it could save you a fortune in fuel. If you regularly drive hundreds of kilometres in one go, test it properly before you sign.

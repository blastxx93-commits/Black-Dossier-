Number three: Suzuki's Black Friday sale.

Suzuki is running a Black Friday sale with ten per cent off the price of black cars. Yes, it only applies to black cars.

The biggest saving, according to CarExpert, is on the new e Vitara Ultra electric SUV: $5,774 off, bringing it to $51,962 drive-away. The e Vitara Motion is $42,862 drive-away, a saving of $4,874. The Vitara Turbo hybrid is $36,762 drive-away, saving $3,974.

And here's the one that'll get attention: the Jimny. The three-door Jimny in black is $35,762 drive-away, a saving of $3,474. The five-door Jimny XL is $37,562 drive-away, saving $3,674. The Swift Hybrid starts at $23,022 drive-away.

The catches are important on this one. It's for private buyers only. You have to buy and take delivery by the 30th of November. It only covers selected new and demonstrator stock at participating dealers. Cars with a black roof and a different body colour don't count, the Jimny Heritage isn't included – and the offer excludes Queensland and northern New South Wales. So if you're in Brisbane, sorry.

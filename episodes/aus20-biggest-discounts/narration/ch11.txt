And number one: the Kia Tasman.

The Tasman is Kia's first-ever ute, and it was developed with Australia in mind. When it was announced, Kia was talking about a target of around twenty thousand sales a year. But according to reports, fewer than four hundred Tasmans were sold in the first nine months of 2026.

So now Kia is cutting hard. According to a report published on the 4th of November, the 2025-model Tasman SX four-wheel drive is now $49,990 drive-away. Its list price was $54,490 before on-road costs, so that's around five thousand dollars off. And the Tasman SX+ four-wheel drive is now $54,990 drive-away, down from $62,390 before on-road costs. Once you add in the on-road costs you'd normally pay, that's a saving of more than ten thousand dollars.

ABN holders can also get five thousand dollars off single and dual cab-chassis S and SX models with an alloy tray.

The catch? The deal is only on 2025 model-year cars, and you have to order and take delivery in November. So stock is limited. And with sales this low, think about resale value. A ute with a small fan base is harder to sell in a few years' time, so plan to keep it for a while – or make sure the discount is big enough to cover the extra depreciation.

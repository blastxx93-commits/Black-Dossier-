Number eight: GWM.

GWM is a Chinese brand, but it's not riding the Chinese boom like BYD or Geely. In October, GWM's sales were basically flat, down 0.3 per cent – the only Chinese brand in the top ten that went backwards. And it's responding with deals.

According to GWM's own offers, listed by CarSorted, the Haval Jolion Premium Hybrid is $32,990 drive-away, with a three-thousand-dollar deposit contribution. The bigger Haval H6 Ultra Hybrid is $43,990 drive-away, also with three thousand dollars towards your deposit. The H6 Lux petrol is $35,990 drive-away with two thousand. And the Cannon ute is from $39,990 drive-away, with a smaller five-hundred-dollar contribution. All of those run until the 30th of November.

Back in October, CarExpert reported GWM offering up to five thousand dollars off selected SUVs. So this brand is clearly in a hurry.

The catch? A deposit contribution is effectively a discount, but it's usually tied to finance or to specific stock. And remember, these deals change month to month. If you miss the end of November, there's a good chance another deal turns up in December – but there's no guarantee it will be the same one.

Number four: the Ford Ranger.

This is Australia's other favourite vehicle. The Ranger has been the best-selling vehicle in the country for four months this year. But it's in a fight with the HiLux, with BYD's Shark 6, and with a wave of new Chinese utes.

So according to CarExpert, Ford is offering a five-thousand-dollar fuel card – or a discount of the same value – on the 2026.5 model year Ranger XLT, Wildtrak and Tremor. For the newer 2027 versions of the same models, the offer is four thousand dollars. And the Ranger Black Edition V6 dual-cab pickup has a drive-away price of $59,000.

Here's the smart move. Ford's offer gives you a choice between a fuel card and a discount. Take the discount. As I said earlier, a discount reduces the price you pay, the amount you borrow and the stamp duty. A fuel card does none of that.

The catch? CarExpert didn't publish an end date for these Ranger offers, so they could end at any time. And the five-thousand-dollar offer is on the older model year. If you're buying to keep for a long time, the model year matters less. If you'll sell it in two or three years, it can matter more.

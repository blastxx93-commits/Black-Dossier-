Number six: BYD's four-thousand-dollar cashback.

BYD is the second biggest brand in the country and it's growing like crazy – up 153 per cent in October. So why is it offering cashback? Because it's in a price war, and it wants to keep the pressure on everyone else.

According to CarExpert, BYD is offering four thousand dollars cashback on the Seal 6, the Shark 6 ute and the Sealion 5, 6, 7 and 8 SUVs. With the cashback, the Sealion 5 Essential works out at $29,990 before on-road costs. The Sealion 7 Premium – one of the best-selling electric cars in the country – works out at $50,990. And the Shark 6 Premium plug-in hybrid ute is $53,900.

There's also 0.99 per cent finance over thirty-six months on most of those models. Orders have to be placed by the 21st of December, with delivery by the end of the year.

The catch? The cashback can't be combined with other BYD offers, it's not for fleet buyers, and demonstrators aren't included. And Zecar reported claims that some dealers are adding another two thousand on top – but BYD hasn't confirmed that. So if a dealer offers you six thousand, get it in writing.

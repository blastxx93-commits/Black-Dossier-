Number five: the Renault Duster.

The Duster is a small, tough SUV, and you can get it with four-wheel drive. Now it's had up to five thousand dollars cut from its price, according to Cars24, and the entry-level Evolution two-wheel drive is now $29,990 drive-away nationwide. That's a real off-road-capable SUV for under thirty thousand dollars drive-away.

The four-wheel-drive versions are around thirty-four to thirty-six thousand drive-away in New South Wales. The offer covers cars bought and delivered between the 1st of November and the 31st of December.

Why the deal? Renault didn't say. But the sales figures do. In October, the whole Renault brand sold just 164 cars in Australia, down 58 per cent on the same month last year. The Arkana sold four, the Koleos 21, and the Kangoo van just two.

The catch? The advertised price is for Solid White only. Any other colour adds $850. And fleet, government and rental buyers can't get the deal. Also think about the brand's future here. With sales this low, ask about the dealer network near you, servicing costs and parts supply before you sign.

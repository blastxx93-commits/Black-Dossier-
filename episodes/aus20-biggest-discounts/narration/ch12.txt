Before we finish, a bonus. This isn't a runout deal, but it's the biggest price cut I found.

Audi has cut the price of the updated Q4 e-tron electric SUV by up to eighteen thousand dollars. According to Cars24, the entry models are now $71,650 before on-road costs, about thirteen thousand dollars cheaper, and the all-wheel-drive quattro versions are up to eighteen thousand dollars cheaper. That brings them well under the luxury car tax threshold.

Why? Because the current Q4 e-tron sold just two cars in Australia in October, down 96 per cent. The catch is that deliveries of the updated model don't start until the first half of 2027.

Number nine: Honda's plate clearance.

Honda sells its cars at fixed, no-haggle prices in Australia. So instead of cutting the price, it's giving away gift cards. Its end-of-year plate clearance, reported by CarExpert, runs until the 31st of January 2027 on 2026-built cars.

The biggest gift card is three thousand dollars, on the CR-V e:HEV X hybrid and the ZR-V e:HEV X hybrid. Other CR-V and ZR-V hybrids get fifteen hundred dollars. And the Civic, the HR-V, the Prelude and the Accord get a thousand dollars.

On top of that, Honda is throwing in the first five scheduled services for free – that's five years or fifty thousand kilometres. CarExpert valued that at $1,395.

So on the best-value models, you're looking at around four thousand dollars in total benefits. Not bad from a brand that almost never does deals.

Here's the catch. Honda's annual service price has just gone up, from $199 to $279. So that free servicing is worth more than it was a month ago. And remember, the gift card arrives within thirty days of delivery – it doesn't come off the price you pay on the day. And if you're thinking about the Accord, it was the number one car in my last video, with just fifty-nine sold in nine months.

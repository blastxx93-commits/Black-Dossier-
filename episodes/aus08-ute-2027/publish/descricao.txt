Toyota Australia says it "cannot rule out price increases" for its utes and 4x4s from 2027. Why? Under the New Vehicle Efficiency Standard (NVES), the CO2 target for utes and vans drops from 180 to 150 g/km in 2027 – and 84% of light commercials sold in Australia are diesel. We explain how the penalties work, what they could add per vehicle, which brands are ahead and behind, the political fight over scrapping the scheme, the electrified utes you can buy instead, and what it means if you're buying a ute in the next 12 months.

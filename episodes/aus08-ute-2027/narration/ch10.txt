So, will ute prices rise in Australia in 2027? Here's the honest answer.

Toyota has openly warned that it can't rule it out. The targets for utes drop from 180 grams to 150 grams in 2027 – one of the biggest single-year jumps in the scheme. Most utes sold in Australia are diesel, and most of them are well above that line. And independent estimates suggest the theoretical penalties could run into thousands of dollars per vehicle by the end of the decade.

And remember, it's not only Toyota's problem. The Ranger, the D-Max, the Triton, the Navara and the BT-50 are all mostly sold as diesels, and every one of those brands faces the same 2027 target.

But it's not a done deal. Car makers can trade credits, change their model mix, and push more hybrids and plug-ins. The NVES is under review. And the opposition has promised to scrap it.

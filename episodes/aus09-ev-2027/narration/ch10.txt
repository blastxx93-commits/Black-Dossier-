So, here's 2027 for Aussie EV drivers in a nutshell.

On the 1st of April 2027, the full fringe benefits tax exemption is limited to EVs costing $75,000 or less. Plug-in hybrids already lost theirs in 2025. In July 2027, NSW plans to start charging EV drivers per kilometre, unless the federal government steps in. Also from July 2027, a new $120,000 luxury car tax threshold is due for zero-emissions vehicles, if the European trade deal is ratified. And tougher emissions targets will keep pushing car makers to sell more EVs – which could mean more discounts.

So, mark these dates. The 31st of March 2027: the last day for the full exemption on EVs over $75,000 in a new novated lease. The 1st of April 2027: the new rules start. The 1st of July 2027: the new luxury car tax threshold for zero-emissions vehicles is due, and the NSW road user charge is planned. And through all of 2027: tougher emissions targets for car makers, and a government review of those rules.

Some of these changes cost money. Some save money. All of them make timing more important than ever.

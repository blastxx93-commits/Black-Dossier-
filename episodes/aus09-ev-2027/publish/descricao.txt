2027 brings the biggest changes yet for Australian EV buyers and owners. From 1 April 2027 the novated-lease FBT exemption (Electric Car Discount) is limited to EVs costing $75,000 or less – the NRMA says some owners could lose more than $17,000 in tax savings. NSW plans a per-kilometre road user charge from July 2027. A new $120,000 luxury car tax threshold for zero-emissions vehicles is due, and tougher emissions targets keep pushing EV prices down. Here's what's confirmed, what's still being decided, and how to time your purchase.

General information only – not financial or tax advice. Check your own situation with your employer, lease provider or tax adviser.

DESCRIÇÃO (copie tudo entre as linhas)
--------------------------------------------------------------
In November 1957, more than fifty of America's most powerful mobsters gathered at a farmhouse in a tiny upstate New York town. One stubborn state trooper noticed. What happened next forced the FBI to admit something it had downplayed for decades: the Mafia was real, and it was national.

This is the story of the Apalachin meeting — the bosses, the roadblock, the run through the woods, and why almost nobody went to prison for it.

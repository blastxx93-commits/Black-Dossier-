Jeep sold 30,408 cars in Australia in 2014 – and just 365 in the first half of 2026. Peugeot's importer is walking away, Jaguar sold zero new cars in September, and Mitsubishi, Subaru and Nissan are down around a quarter while facing emissions penalties. With around 70 brands fighting for 1.2 million sales, which car brands are under the most pressure heading into 2027 – and what happens to your warranty, servicing, parts and resale value if your brand leaves?

Important: none of the brands in this video has announced it is leaving Australia, and several say they are committed to the market. This video looks at published sales data and what owners should know.

Real 2026 news. No rumours.

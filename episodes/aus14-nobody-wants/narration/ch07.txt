Number four: the Alfa Romeo Tonale and Stelvio.

Alfa Romeo has one of the most passionate fan bases in the car world. But passion doesn't pay the bills. In September, the Tonale small SUV registered two. The bigger Stelvio also registered two. The updated Tonale starts at $64,990 before on-road costs – for a small SUV. Alfa has run deals with more than ten thousand dollars off in the past, and the brand as a whole sold just 21 cars that month.

Think about that for a moment. Two Tonales in a month, while a single Chinese small SUV, the Chery Tiggo 4, sold more than two thousand. Brand heritage counts for a lot less than it used to.

Number three: the Fiat 500.

The cute little Fiat 500 was once a common sight in every city. In September 2026, the Fiat 500 and Abarth line found two buyers, down almost 95 per cent. Fiat already tried the price-cut route with the electric 500e. In 2025, it dropped the 500e La Prima from $52,500 before on-road costs to $38,990 drive-away. Fiat said the deal was to "stimulate sales and clear out existing stock." When a brand says the words "clear out existing stock" out loud, you know things are tough.

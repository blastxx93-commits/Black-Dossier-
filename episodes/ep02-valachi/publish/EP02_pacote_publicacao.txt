DESCRIÇÃO (copie tudo entre as linhas)
--------------------------------------------------------------
In June 1962, inside the Atlanta Federal Penitentiary, a Mafia soldier killed the wrong man. Fifteen months later, guarded by two hundred federal marshals, he sat in front of the United States Senate and the television cameras, and said the words his bosses swore would never be spoken: Cosa Nostra.

This is the story of Joseph Valachi — thirty years a soldier in the Genovese family, the "kiss of death" from Vito Genovese, and the testimony that gave America its first map of the Mafia from the inside.

September 27th, 1963.

Washington, D.C. A government car pulls up to the United States Capitol. Around it, roughly two hundred federal marshals.

They are not guarding a president. They are not guarding a king.

They are guarding a career criminal pushing sixty, a man with a gravel voice and a prison haircut. A man who, a year earlier, had killed a stranger in a prison yard because he was sure the stranger had come to kill him.

Inside a Senate hearing room, television cameras are waiting. Millions of Americans are about to hear something no member of the Mafia had ever said out loud, in public, under oath.

That the organization was real. That it had a name. That it had ranks, rules and rituals. And that once you were in, you could never get out.

His bosses had put a price on his head. One hundred thousand dollars, he said.

He talked anyway.

This is Black Dossier. And this... is the Valachi file.

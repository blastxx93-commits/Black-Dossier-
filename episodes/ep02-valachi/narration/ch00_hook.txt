June 1962. Atlanta Federal Penitentiary.

A man in prison grays picks up a length of iron pipe.

He has not slept properly in weeks. He is sure that somewhere in this yard, someone has been sent to kill him. And he has just recognized the face.

He swings.

The man on the ground is a stranger. Not a hit man. Not a mobster. Just the wrong face, in the wrong place.

The killer's name is Joe Valachi. Thirty years a soldier in one of the most powerful crime families in America. And in that moment, he has nowhere left to go.

His own boss wants him dead. The government wants him for murder.

So he does the one thing no made man had ever done.

He talks.

Fifteen months later, under the guard of two hundred federal marshals, on live television, in front of the United States Senate, he will say the words his bosses swore would never be spoken.

Cosa Nostra. Our thing.

This is Black Dossier. And this... is the Valachi file.

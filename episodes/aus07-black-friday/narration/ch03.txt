So what's on offer right now, heading into November? Here's what CarsGuide reported in October 2026.

GWM was celebrating eighteen years in Australia with cuts across its range. The Haval Jolion Premium was down three thousand dollars to $23,990 drive-away. The Haval H6 hybrid Lux was $37,990, three thousand dollars off. And the Cannon Alpha diesel Ultra ute was four thousand dollars off, at $54,990. Those deals were advertised until the 31st of October.

Chery offered three thousand dollars cashback on the Tiggo 8 plug-in hybrid, now $42,990 drive-away, and on the seven-seat Tiggo 9 plug-in hybrid, now $49,990 drive-away. It also offered fuel cards worth up to two thousand dollars on other Tiggo models.

Geely offered six per cent off the EX2, EX5 and Starray EM-i – but only for buyers using a novated lease, and before on-road costs.

At the premium end, Mercedes-Benz advertised the GLC 200 from $79,990 drive-away, compared with a price of around $89,000 before on-road costs, as long as you order by the 30th of November and take delivery before the end of the year. And Skoda was offering the Kodiaq mild hybrid from $49,990 drive-away until the end of December – effectively covering the on-road costs.

Now, these aren't Black Friday deals. Many of them ended on the 31st of October. But they show you the starting point. If a brand's "Black Friday price" in late November is the same as its October price, that's not a Black Friday deal. That's just the price.

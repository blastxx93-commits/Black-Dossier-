Four and a half billion dollars. That's how much Finder estimates Australians spend on Black Friday. And this year, Black Friday lands on the 27th of November 2026, with Cyber Monday on the 30th.

For years, Black Friday was about TVs, phones and air fryers. Not anymore. Car brands have jumped in. Last November, Suzuki, Omoda Jaecoo, Geely, Chery and Isuzu all ran Black Friday car deals in Australia – cashback, free servicing, cheap finance, thousands off drive-away prices.

But here's the problem. When Savvy looked at last year's Black Friday car sales, it found that at least one big brand's "Black Friday" campaign was really just a demo clearance. And some of the cheapest-looking finance offers came with deposits of up to thirty-five per cent, or a big balloon payment at the end.

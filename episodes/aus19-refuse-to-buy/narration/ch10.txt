Number two: the Kia EV9.

This is the most expensive car on the list. The Kia EV9 is a big, seven-seat electric SUV, and in 2026 Kia added a new GT version priced at $129,250 – the most expensive Kia ever sold in Australia. The range starts at $97,000 before on-road costs.

And here's how many Australians bought one in the first nine months of 2026: 120. That's down from 238 in the same period last year – a drop of almost 50 per cent. Half the buyers, gone in a year.

GoAuto compared it with the biggest seller in its class, the Toyota LandCruiser 300, which sold 9,787. That's more than eighty-one LandCruisers for every EV9.

So what went wrong? Electric car sales are booming – but they're booming at the cheaper end. The best-selling EVs are thirty, forty and fifty thousand dollar cars. A hundred-thousand-dollar electric family bus is a very small market, and it's now shared with Chinese seven-seaters that cost a fraction of the price. And remember, the Hyundai Ioniq 9 – the EV9's sister under the skin – sold just four cars in October.

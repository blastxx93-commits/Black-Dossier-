First, the big picture, because it explains everything on this list.

Australians bought 107,493 new vehicles in October 2026, according to the official VFACTS figures. That's up 6.8 per cent on October last year, and a record for the month. Year to date, the market has already passed one million sales.

But look at where that money is going. Electric car sales were up 237 per cent on October last year. BYD sold more than ten thousand cars in a single month, up 153 per cent, and it's now firmly the second biggest brand in the country, behind only Toyota. Geely was up 520 per cent. Cars built in China were up 104 per cent.

Now look at who's going backwards. Mitsubishi, down 50 per cent for the month. Volkswagen, down 46. Audi, down 45. Subaru, down 42. Hyundai, down almost 29. Mazda, down almost 20. Cars from Japan were down 13 per cent, from Korea down 23, and from Germany down 24.

So this isn't a weak market. It's a market where buyers are moving fast – and when buyers move this fast, some cars get left behind in the dealer's yard.

A quick word on how I built this list. The ranking uses year-to-date sales from January to September 2026, compared with the same nine months of 2025, as compiled by GoAuto from the official VFACTS data. I've added October numbers from CarExpert's sales database where they help. And I've only included cars that are still on sale. Cars that have already been axed are a different story, and I'll deal with those separately.

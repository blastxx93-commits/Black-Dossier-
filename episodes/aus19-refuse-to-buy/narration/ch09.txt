Number three: the Skoda Fabia.

Skoda number three. And yes, that means one brand has three cars in this top ten.

The Fabia is Skoda's smallest car, a little European hatchback. It sold 140 cars in the first nine months of 2026, down from 246 in the same period last year. That's a fall of 43 per cent.

Now look at the car that's eating its lunch. In the light car segment, the MG3 sold 6,340. That's more than forty-five MG3s for every Fabia. A cheap Chinese-made hybrid hatch has completely taken over the space that European small cars used to own.

And the Skoda pattern goes further. In October, the Skoda Superb – one of the roomiest cars you can buy – sold nine. The Skoda Scala small hatch sold zero. Skoda's sister brand Volkswagen was down 46 per cent for the month, and Cupra was down 67.

To be fair to Skoda, it's pushing new models like the Elroq electric SUV and new hybrids. But right now, the old guard of European small cars is getting crushed from every direction.

Number eight: the Jeep Wrangler.

The Wrangler is an icon. It's the original off-roader, and you can spot one from a mile away. But in the first nine months of 2026, Australians bought just 247 of them, down from 345 in the same period last year. That's a fall of more than 28 per cent.

Price is the big issue. The cheapest four-door Wrangler went from around thirty-seven thousand dollars back in 2014 to almost seventy-six thousand before on-road costs. At that money, buyers are cross-shopping a Prado, an Everest, or a Chinese four-by-four with a lot more equipment.

GoAuto worked out that the Ford Everest outsold the Wrangler by more than sixty-five to one this year.

And look at the rest of the Jeep showroom. In October, the Jeep Compass sold one car. One. The Grand Cherokee and the Avenger sold zero. Right now, Jeep is offering a four-thousand-dollar deposit contribution on the Wrangler Rubicon four-door, according to the deals tracker CarSorted. When a brand starts paying part of your deposit, that tells you how hard the cars are to move.

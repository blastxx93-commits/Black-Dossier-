Number ten: the Mazda CX-70.

The CX-70 is Mazda's big, premium five-seat SUV, with a powerful six-cylinder engine. It was designed mainly for the American market, and Mazda brought it to Australia hoping it would sit at the top of its range.

It didn't work. In the first nine months of 2026, the CX-70 sold just 182 cars in Australia, down almost 35 per cent on the same period last year. In the same class, the Land Rover Defender sold more than three thousand. That's seventeen Defenders for every CX-70.

And this is after a big price cut. In July 2026, Mazda cut CX-70 prices by between $3,700 and $8,051, depending on the variant. Sales still didn't recover.

Then in late October, Mazda confirmed that the CX-70 is ending production for the United States – its biggest market. Mazda Australia says the car will continue here. But when a car is built for America and America stops getting it, that's a warning sign for anyone buying one in Australia. Mazda as a whole was down almost 20 per cent in October.

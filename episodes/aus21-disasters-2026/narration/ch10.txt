Number two: the Hyundai Palisade.

The new-generation Palisade is Hyundai's big seven- and eight-seat family SUV. It launched in Australia with a lot of excitement. Then, in March 2026, came the news no car company ever wants.

On the 7th of March, a two-year-old child died in an incident involving a Palisade in Ohio, in the United States. Hyundai said the power-operated second- and third-row seats in some models "may not adequately detect contact with an occupant or object as intended".

Hyundai halted sales and deliveries of the affected Palisade in North America, Korea – and Australia. CarExpert reported around 2,260 vehicles were affected here. On the 2nd of April, a formal recall was issued in Australia for 2,242 Palisades from the 2025 and 2026 model years, to fix the software in the electric seat-folding system. The fix is free, and owners can get it at a dealer or, in some cases, over the air.

This is a tragedy first, and a business disaster second. If you own an affected Palisade, check that the recall work has been done – and, as Hyundai advises, always make sure no person or object is in the seat's path before folding it.

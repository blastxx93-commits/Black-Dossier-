Number five: Nissan.

This is a brand that sold almost eighty thousand cars a year in Australia at its peak in 2012. In 2026, it's having its worst year in a very long time.

From January to July, Nissan delivered 15,445 vehicles, down almost 34 per cent. The Qashqai was down 73 per cent. And the new-generation Navara ute – a car that should have been a fresh start – was down almost 49 per cent.

Then came the cuts. Nissan axed the Juke and the Pathfinder in Australia. The new-generation Leaf electric car was postponed indefinitely. And CarExpert reported that a tougher Navara Warrior model was cancelled.

Nissan's Oceania boss, Steve Milette, was remarkably honest about it. He told CarExpert: "2026 is the low point, and it then increases from there on in." In other words, Nissan itself is calling 2026 the bottom.

To be fair to Nissan, there's a plan. It's lining up new models, including Chinese-built ones, for 2027. But for 2026, the numbers speak for themselves.

Before the countdown, a quick look at the battlefield, because it explains almost every disaster on this list.

Australians bought 107,493 new vehicles in October 2026, a record for the month, and the market passed one million sales for the year by the end of October. But the money is moving fast. Cars built in China were up 104 per cent in October. Electric cars were up 237 per cent. BYD sold more than ten thousand cars in a single month, and Geely was up 520 per cent.

At the same time, cars from Japan were down 13 per cent, from Korea down 23, and from Germany down 24. Mitsubishi was down 50 per cent in October, Volkswagen down 46, Audi down 45 and Subaru down 42.

In a market moving this fast, a car doesn't have to be bad to become a disaster. It just has to be too expensive, too late, or in the wrong place at the wrong time. And in 2026, plenty of cars managed all three.

A quick word on how I chose this list. I've looked at sales collapses, cancellations, recalls and broken promises, using official VFACTS sales data and reports from CarExpert, GoAuto and others. I'm ranking them by how big the gap is between what was expected and what actually happened. Let's go.

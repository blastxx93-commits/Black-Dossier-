Number seven: Maserati.

Maserati is one of the most famous names in the car world – the trident badge, the Italian V6 and V8 engines, the luxury. But in Australia in 2026, it's in freefall.

According to CarExpert's analysis of the first half of the year, Maserati sold just 101 cars in six months, down 33 per cent. Its main model, the Grecale SUV, sold 98. For comparison, the Genesis GV70 luxury SUV alone sold 570 in the same period. The GranTurismo and GranCabrio sold three between them.

And the range is shrinking. The Ghibli sedan, the Levante SUV and the Quattroporte limousine have all been discontinued. In October, the Ghibli and the Levante recorded zero sales.

When a luxury brand stops selling, it's not just a problem for the brand. It's a problem for owners. Resale values fall, dealers get fewer customers, and buyers start to worry about parts and servicing. That's how a brand slowly disappears from a market – one quiet month at a time.

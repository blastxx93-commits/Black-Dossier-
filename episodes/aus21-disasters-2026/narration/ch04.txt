Number eight: the Honda Accord.

This is the one that shocked a lot of people in my last countdown. In the first nine months of 2026, Honda sold just 59 Accords in the whole of Australia. That's fewer than seven a month.

At around $64,900 drive-away for the single hybrid RS model, it's an expensive mid-size sedan in a market that's walking away from sedans. In the same period, Toyota sold 9,471 Camrys – more than one hundred and sixty Camrys for every Accord.

Honda moved to a fixed-price agency model in 2021, with fewer dealerships and no haggling. It said it wanted fewer, more profitable sales. With the Accord, it certainly got fewer.

Now Honda is trying to clear 2026-built stock with a thousand-dollar gift card and five free services on the Accord. And CarExpert reported the updated 2027 Accord will cost a thousand dollars more. To be fair, GoAuto pointed out that low sales like these are not necessarily about product quality – and the Accord is a well-reviewed car. But fifty-nine sales in nine months is still a disaster by any measure.

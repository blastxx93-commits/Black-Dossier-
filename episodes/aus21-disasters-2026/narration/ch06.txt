Number six: Peugeot.

In July 2026, Peugeot's Australian importer, Inchcape, announced that it was ending its distribution deal for the brand. In CarExpert's words, Peugeot was "dumped by its Australian distributor".

The sales numbers explain why. Peugeot's best year in Australia was 2007, with 8,807 sales. In 2025, it was down to 1,350. In the first five months of 2026, it delivered just 373 vehicles – and its best seller was the Partner van. In the first half of the year, the Peugeot 208 small car recorded just one delivery. One.

And in October, the Peugeot 308 sold three cars and the 508 sold none.

Stellantis, Peugeot's parent company, says it intends to "maintain continuity of distribution in Australia". Inchcape says it will keep looking after existing owners during the transition. But this is the second French brand Inchcape has walked away from, after Citroen in 2024 – and Citroen is no longer sold new in Australia. If you own a Peugeot, keep an eye on where your nearest service centre will be.

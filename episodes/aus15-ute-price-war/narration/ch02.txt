So let's look at the deals, starting with the big one: Toyota.

Toyota is offering a $4,000 "deposit bonus" on new and demonstrator 2026 HiLux SR5 models – the four-wheel-drive versions. CarsGuide reported that the SR5 range starts at $69,901 drive-away in New South Wales, rising to $73,952 for the top double-cab cab-chassis with the new 48-volt mild-hybrid system.

But read the fine print. The bonus only applies if you finance the ute through Toyota Finance, with at least ten thousand dollars borrowed. Finance applications have to be in between the 1st of September and the 31st of December 2026, and the car has to be settled by the 28th of February 2027. It covers private buyers and some smaller fleet customers, and it excludes the electric HiLux.

Why is Toyota doing this? Its sales boss, John Pappas, said Toyota is "targeting a very strong, record fourth quarter sales for 2026," thanks to better vehicle supply. In plain English: Toyota has more utes coming, it wants to move them, and it wants to stop the Ranger from running away with the title.

And remember, this is a brand-new HiLux. The new generation arrived in late 2025, with a mild-hybrid diesel and even a fully electric version. Normally, a new model sells strongly in its first year. Instead, the HiLux was down almost twelve per cent in September. Toyota needs this one to work.

That's worth remembering. When the market leader starts offering finance bonuses on its most iconic model, it's not doing it for fun.

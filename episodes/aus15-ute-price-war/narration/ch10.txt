So, are ute prices collapsing in Australia? Not across the board – but the discounting is the biggest we've seen in years, and it's coming from the very top. Toyota is offering $4,000 on the HiLux. Ford cut the Ranger Hybrid by more than ten thousand dollars. Kia slashed up to fifteen thousand off the Tasman. And BYD is throwing $4,000 cashback at the Shark 6.

The reason is simple: Australians are buying fewer traditional utes, and every brand is fighting over the buyers who are left. For you, that means bargaining power – at least for now.

Here's what to watch next. The October sales figures land in early November, and they'll show whether Toyota's HiLux bonus is working. The race between the Ranger and the HiLux for the 2026 crown will go down to the wire. Black Friday and the end-of-year sales push will bring another wave of deals. And in 2027, tougher emissions targets and a flood of new plug-in utes will shake things up all over again. Whatever you drive, the next six months could be one of the best windows in a decade to negotiate on a ute.

So how do you get the best deal in the middle of a ute price war?

First, compare drive-away prices, not headlines. A "$4,000 bonus" and a "$4,000 fuel card" aren't the same as $4,000 off the price. Ask every dealer for a written drive-away price, including all accessories you actually need.

Second, read the finance conditions. The HiLux bonus requires Toyota Finance and a minimum amount borrowed. That can still be a good deal – but compare the interest rate and the total cost of the loan with your bank. A bonus that's swallowed by a higher rate isn't a bonus.

Third, check the build date and look at demonstrators. Toyota's offer covers demonstrators, and many brands have runout stock. Older build dates should mean a sharper price.

Fourth, think about resale. Toyota and Ford utes have historically held their value strongly. A heavily discounted ute from a brand with weaker resale might cost you more when you sell. Look at the total cost over the years you'll keep it, not just the price on day one.

Fifth, use the calendar. Black Friday falls on the 27th of November, BYD's cashback runs to the 21st of December, and the HiLux finance window closes on the 31st of December. Dealers chasing end-of-year targets are often at their most flexible in the final weeks of December.

And finally, buy the ute you actually need. If you tow a heavy caravan every weekend, a cheap plug-in hybrid with a lower towing capacity might not suit you, however good the deal. If you mostly drive in the suburbs, ask yourself whether you need a ute at all.

So should you buy now, or wait? If you need a ute in the next few months, this is one of the strongest buyer's markets in years. Toyota, Ford, Kia and BYD are all chasing volume before the end of 2026, and some of these deals end in December. But if you can wait, keep an eye on 2027. New plug-in and electric utes are arriving, and if the emissions rules push prices up, the deals could dry up quickly. Nobody can promise which way prices will go – but you'll be in a much stronger position if you know the numbers before you walk into a dealership.

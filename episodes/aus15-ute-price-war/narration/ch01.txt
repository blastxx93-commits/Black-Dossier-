Let's start with the numbers, because they're the reason all of this is happening.

In September 2026, the Ford Ranger was Australia's best-selling vehicle, with 5,062 sales, up four per cent. But the Toyota HiLux fell to fourth, with 4,455 – down 11.7 per cent. Even more telling, the Toyota RAV4 SUV, with 4,624 sales, outsold the HiLux. Not long ago, that would have been unthinkable.

For the year to date, the race for Australia's number one vehicle is incredibly tight. The Ranger is on 37,858. The HiLux is on 36,616. That's a gap of just over twelve hundred, with three months to go. And winning that crown matters enormously for marketing, for dealers and for pride.

Back in May, CarsGuide's news editor, Dom Tripolone, put it bluntly: "We've hit peak ute and the only way is down." Four-wheel-drive ute sales were already falling then, and the decline has continued.

It's a remarkable turnaround. For most of the past decade, the Ranger and the HiLux took turns as the country's best-selling vehicle. Toyota's former sales and marketing boss, Sean Hanley, saw this coming back at the start of 2025. He said "the number of ute models available to Australian buyers will expand rapidly", and that an SUV could rise to the top of the national sales charts "in the next year or two". He was right on both counts.

So what's driving buyers away? Three big things.

First, fuel. In May 2026, diesel reached as high as $3.50 a litre in some parts of Australia, according to CarExpert. Fill an eighty-litre ute tank at that price and you're up for about $280.

Second, the government's emissions rules. Under the New Vehicle Efficiency Standard, car makers face penalties for selling too many high-emitting vehicles – and diesel utes are some of the highest emitters on the market.

Third, the alternatives have exploded. Plug-in hybrid utes, Chinese utes, and family SUVs with hybrid and electric power. Chinese-built vehicles made up more than thirty-five per cent of all new-car sales in September. Buyers who once defaulted to a dual-cab now have a dozen other options.

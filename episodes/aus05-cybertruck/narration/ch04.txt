So what do Aussie reservation holders actually get?

Tesla says reservations will be cancelled and deposits refunded in full. Customers will get a separate email to confirm their refund details, and EV Central reports the money should arrive within thirty days.

And Tesla has added a sweetener. Reservation holders who order a new Model 3 or Model Y get one year of Full Self-Driving – Supervised – for free. At the current price of $149 a month, that's worth $1,788.

Now, it's worth being clear about what that is. "Supervised" means exactly that. The driver must stay alert, keep their hands ready and remain fully responsible for the car at all times. It's a driver assistance feature, not a car that drives itself.

If you paid a deposit back in 2019 and you've changed your email address, or you can't find the message, contact Tesla directly. A $150 refund isn't life-changing, but it's your money, and after seven years you're entitled to it.

On 23 October 2026, Tesla emailed Australian Cybertruck reservation holders: plans to produce the Cybertruck for Australia and New Zealand have been discontinued. Almost seven years after the $150 deposits of November 2019, here's what happened – the roadshow, the promises, the collapsing US sales, the price, the right-hand-drive problem and 11 US recalls – plus how the refund works, why Tesla itself is having a record 2026 in Australia, and which American and electric utes Aussies can actually buy instead.

Real 2026 news. No rumours.

At number six, the Isuzu D-Max. The D-Max is the steady achiever – tough, simple, reliable, and loved in the bush. In February it held onto third spot among utes, and in July it was still the third best-selling 4x4 ute with 1,292 sales.

So why is it here? Because the pressure is coming from underneath. The BYD Shark 6 has been trading blows with the D-Max for that third spot, and more electrified utes are on the way. GAC has confirmed its ute will be electrified only, and Chery's diesel-electric Stockman is due in late 2026. The D-Max is a diesel ute in a market that is slowly turning away from diesel. It's not falling yet – but it's standing on shifting ground. For the farmers and tradies who just want a simple diesel that runs forever, the D-Max is still a favourite – and with competition this fierce, it may be easier to negotiate on than it was a few years ago.

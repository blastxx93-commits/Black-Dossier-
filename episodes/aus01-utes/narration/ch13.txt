So what should you do if you need a ute? First, this is a buyer's market. Dealers are carrying stock, brands are running drive-away deals, and loyalty bonuses are back. Don't pay sticker price.

Second, check the run-out stock. When a new model year arrives – like the MY26.5 Ranger – the outgoing stock is often where the best deals are.

Third, think about resale. Heavy discounting on a new model can drag down used values later. If you're buying a ute that's struggling to sell new, expect it to be worth less when you trade it in.

And finally, deals change every month. Everything we've covered here was correct when it was announced, but always confirm the current drive-away price with the dealer before you sign anything.

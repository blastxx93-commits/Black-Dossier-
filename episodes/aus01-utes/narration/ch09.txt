At number four, the Nissan Navara. This one hurts, because the Navara has been an Aussie favourite for decades.

Nissan as a brand had a rough 2025. Sales fell 21.6 per cent, and the old Navara fell 28 per cent. The new-generation Navara – that Triton twin we mentioned – was supposed to turn it around. But in May 2026 it managed 217 sales. In July, 212.

So Nissan reached for the price lever. Until the end of August, the base Navara was offered at $49,990 drive-away, down from $53,348 before on-road costs. The top Pro-4X dropped to $63,990 drive-away from $68,418 plus on-roads. Existing Nissan owners were offered a $2,000 loyalty bonus on top. Those deals have now ended, but they tell you exactly how hard Nissan is pushing. A brand-new model being discounted this heavily, this early, is a warning sign.

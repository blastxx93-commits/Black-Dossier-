Number two is the Toyota HiLux. Yes, the HiLux. For years it was untouchable.

In early 2026, Toyota as a whole was down around 25 per cent, which Toyota blamed on supply problems during model changeovers. The HiLux was down 19.1 per cent year-on-year in May. Through the first part of the year, about 2,600 fewer HiLuxes found homes compared to 2025.

There's also a product question. The latest HiLux is a heavy update rather than an all-new ute, and rivals have been quick to point that out. Toyota also launched a HiLux electric ute, but with a fairly short range it's a niche product – only fourteen were delivered in May.

Now, here's the twist. In July 2026, the HiLux actually beat the Ranger, 4,119 to 3,827, likely because new stock finally arrived. So the HiLux isn't dying. But Toyota fighting stock shortages while the whole segment shrinks means every month is now a battle.

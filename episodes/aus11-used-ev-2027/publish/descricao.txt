Used electric car prices in Australia could be heading for their biggest test yet. Thousands of EVs bought on novated leases since the 2022 tax break are now coming back to market, and auction house Pickles says its ex-novated lease volumes were up 157% in the September quarter of 2026. So will used EV prices crash in 2027 – or is this the best time in years to buy? Here are the real 2026 numbers on used EV sales, prices, depreciation and battery health, plus a checklist for sellers and buyers.

This video uses published data and attributed quotes. Nobody can promise where prices go next – this is about understanding the forces at play.
